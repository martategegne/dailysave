Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

1. Information We Collect

DailySave collects only the minimal data required to provide personal budgeting and synchronization services:

  • Account Data: Email address and cryptographically salted password hash (Argon2id).
  • Ledger Data: Financial transaction amounts, dates, categories, budget allocations, and debt records that you enter into the application.
  • Community Equb Data: Member contribution records for groups you voluntarily create or join.

2. Local-First Storage & Data Sovereignty

Your primary financial database lives on your local device using Drift (SQLite on native platforms, IndexedDB in the browser). You can utilize DailySave completely offline without sending data to any remote server.

3. Cloud Synchronization & Encryption

When you enable cloud backup, your transactions are transmitted exclusively over encrypted HTTPS (TLS 1.2/1.3) to our private PostgreSQL cluster. All database access is strictly multi-tenant isolated by your user ID.

4. Zero Third-Party Monetization

We do not sell, rent, monetize, or trade your personal financial data to advertisers, data brokers, or credit scoring agencies.

5. Data Deletion & Export

You have full rights to export your complete financial history to open CSV or PDF formats at any time. Upon request or account deletion, all remote server records are permanently purged.
