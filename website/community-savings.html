Equb & Community Savings Groups

Digitize your traditional Ethiopian Equb (ዕቁብ) and community savings circles with transparent contribution ledgers and ironclad personal privacy.

Our Privacy & Non-Custodial Commitment

DailySave is strictly an informational and organizational ledger. We do not hold, transmit, or custody member funds. Furthermore, individual members' personal transaction histories, accounts, and private budgets are never revealed to other group members.

Role-Based Control

Assign group Creator, Admin, Treasurer, and Member roles with granular permissions for recording pool contributions and expenses.

Equb Contribution Cycles

Set weekly, bi-weekly, or monthly rounds. Track who has paid, who is pending, and who has received the cycle payout.

Shared Financial Reports

Instant transparent reporting on the total group pool, total member payouts, and communal operational fees.
