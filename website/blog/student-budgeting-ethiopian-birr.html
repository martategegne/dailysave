Student Budgeting 101: Stretching Your Birr on Campus

By DailySave Education Team • 5 min read

University Life on a Fixed Allowance

Whether living on campus at 4 Kilo, 5 Kilo, Jimma, or Hawassa, college life introduces financial independence all at once. Without a structured system, allowances sent by family or earned via freelance tutoring can disappear in the first 10 days of the month.

The 50/30/20 Campus Rule

Adapt the classic budgeting rule to university life:

  • 50% Essentials: Photocopying, course materials, dorm toiletries, and basic food supplements.
  • 30% Campus Life & Social: Tea/coffee with classmates, group study snacks, mobile internet packages.
  • 20% Future Cushion: Unforeseen medical trips, semester-end travel home, and graduation project savings.
