The Modern Equb Guide: How to Organize Rotating Savings Circles

By DailySave Community Team • 6 min read

What is an Equb (ዕቁብ)?

Equb is Ethiopia's traditional rotating savings and credit association (ROSCA). A fixed group of trusted participants contributes a set amount of Birr at regular intervals (daily, weekly, or monthly). At each interval, the aggregated pool is awarded to one member according to a lottery draw or predetermined schedule until every participant has received a turn.

Three Keys to a Successful Modern Equb

  1. Role Clarity: Appoint a dedicated Secretary/Treasurer who logs contributions and shared administrative fees transparently.
  2. Definite Payment Rules: Establish clear contribution deadlines (e.g. within 24 hours of month-end) and agreed procedures if a member defaults.
  3. Individual Privacy: Members join an Equb to build collective capital, not to expose their personal banking history. Ensure your tracking system never leaks private member transactions.
