About DailySave (ስለ የዕለት ቁጠባ)

Empowering households, students, and businesses with financial clarity, mathematical certainty, and inclusive design.

Our Mission

Managing money should not require an expensive smartphone, reliable high-speed broadband, or advanced accounting training. In Ethiopia and East Africa, daily financial transactions occur continuously in markets, transport, and community groups, often in environments with intermittent internet connectivity.

DailySave was engineered from the ground up to be offline-first, accessible, and mathematically verifiable. Every Birr is tracked in exact minor units (cents), keeping users in complete command of their cash flow.

Our Core Principles

  • Offline-First Resilience: Your financial data belongs on your device first. Drift SQLite guarantees local availability.
  • WCAG 2.2 AA Accessibility: Screen readers, high contrast, and large touch targets ensure blind, low-vision, and motor-impaired individuals can manage their money independently.
  • Cultural Authenticity: Native integration of the Ethiopian Ge'ez calendar, Ge'ez numerals, Amharic, Afaan Oromo, and traditional Equb community circles.
  • Strict Privacy: Zero money-holding, zero third-party data selling, and zero ungrounded AI hallucination.
