# DailySave Business Model & Monetization Strategy

DailySave operates on a sustainable, ethical, and transparent **freemium SaaS model** designed for the socioeconomic context of Ethiopia and East Africa. 

---

## 1. Monetization Pillars & Tiers

```
+---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|                                    DailySave Tiers                                    |
+-------------------+-------------------+-------------------+---------------------------+
|      FREE         |       PLUS        |      FAMILY       |   BUSINESS / COMMUNITY    |
|      0 ETB        |    150 ETB/mo     |    250 ETB/mo     |     500 / 350 ETB/mo      |
+-------------------+-------------------+-------------------+---------------------------+
| Core Offline App  | AI Trend Insights | Shared Budgets    | Multi-Staff Cash Register |
| Basic Budgets     | Unlimited Goals   | Joint Savings     | Accountant Access         |
| 1 Equb (<= 20)    | Priority Cloud    | Role Management   | Contribution Schedules    |
+-------------------+-------------------+-------------------+---------------------------+
```

### A. Individual Free Tier (0 ETB / Forever)
* **Target:** University students, individuals tracking everyday pocket expenses.
* **Included:** Offline-first recording, 64-bit integer net savings calculation, Ethiopian Ge'ez calendar, recurring bills, 1 Equb circle (up to 20 members), PDF/CSV statements.

### B. DailySave Plus (150 ETB / Month or 1,500 ETB / Year)
* **Target:** Young professionals and salary earners seeking proactive wealth-building habits.
* **Included:** Advanced spending trend analytics, Pareto category concentration analysis, unlimited savings goals, enhanced multi-device sync, and optional spending pattern insights.

### C. Family Financial Circle (250 ETB / Month or 2,500 ETB / Year)
* **Target:** Households managing joint grocery budgets, utility vouchers, and tuition fees.
* **Included:** Shared family workspace, multi-member permission controls, joint goal progress, and household spending summaries.

### D. Small Business & Merchant Plan (500 ETB / Month or 5,000 ETB / Year)
* **Target:** Retail shops, cafes, pharmacies, and small service providers.
* **Included:** Multi-account cash register (Telebirr, CBE Birr, Cash), staff cashier permissions, external accountant audit access, cashflow reports, and supplier/customer debt tracking.

### E. Community Equb Organizer (350 ETB / Month or 3,500 ETB / Year)
* **Target:** Equb chairpersons, social associations, and community treasurers.
* **Included:** Unlimited concurrent Equb circles, automated in-app contribution schedules, role-based treasurer records, and transparent payout audit statements.

---

## 2. Institutional & Enterprise Expansion

1. **Microfinance & SACCO Partnerships:** Licensing DailySave to Ethiopian Savings and Credit Cooperatives (SACCOs) to promote financial literacy among borrowers.
2. **University Campus Programs:** Offering sponsored Plus features to accredited universities for student financial empowerment.
3. **White-Label Licensing:** Providing a customized, privately deployed version of the DailySave offline-first money engine for corporate payroll and staff expense management.

---

## 3. Core Business Principles

> [!IMPORTANT]
> 1. **Zero Data Monetization:** We never sell, rent, or monetize private transaction logs, user spending habits, or financial balances.
> 2. **Financial Data Sovereignty:** Users maintain complete access to export or delete their records at any time.
> 3. **Transparent Pricing:** No hidden fees, unexpected recurring charges, or dark-pattern cancellations.
