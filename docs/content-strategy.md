# DailySave Content & SEO Strategy (Ethiopian Personal Finance)

This strategy document outlines organic search content pillars, audience personas, a 12-month editorial calendar, and grounded financial literacy themes for DailySave.

---

## 1. Audience Personas & Search Intent

| Persona | Primary Challenges | Search Intent & Questions | Core Product Value |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Urban Salary Earner** (Addis Ababa, Hawassa, Adama) | Rapid inflation, rising rent, multiple mobile wallets (Telebirr, CBE Birr) | *"How to save money in Ethiopian Birr"*, *"Best personal budgeting app Ethiopia"* | Multi-wallet consolidation, integer-exact net savings tracking |
| **University Student** (AAU, Jimma, Bahir Dar) | Limited monthly stipend, high cafeteria and printing costs | *"How to budget university allowance"*, *"Student savings tips in Ethiopia"* | 100% free, lightweight, zero mobile data needed |
| **Household Budget Manager** | Groceries, utility vouchers, school tuition, family health emergencies | *"Monthly family food budget planner"*, *"Managing household expenses in Birr"* | Recurring bill reminders, visual budget threshold alerts |
| **Equb Organizer / Chairperson** | Tracking contribution turns, paper receipts lost, member payment disputes | *"How to organize Equb spreadsheet"*, *"Equb contribution tracking app"* | Role-based member records, transparent payout audits, privacy isolation |
| **Small Shop Owner / Merchant** | Mixed personal and business cash, customer credit ledger | *"Daily sales and expense record app"*, *"Simple shop ledger Ethiopia"* | Offline transaction recording, customer debt separation, CSV exports |

## 2. Core Content Pillars

### Pillar A: Ethiopian Birr Cashflow & Daily Savings
* **Focus:** Establishing the habit of daily expense recording rather than periodic monthly guessing.
* **Topics:** The `Savings = Income - Expenses` rule, eliminating small daily spending leaks (coffee, transport, snacks), and maintaining purchasing power.

### Pillar B: Traditional Equb & Modern Community Finance
* **Focus:** Bridging traditional community social capital with digital transparency.
* **Topics:** How to run an Equb transparently, establishing rules for contribution rounds, and why personal transaction privacy is vital in communal groups.

### Pillar C: Seasonal Budgeting & Ethiopian Holiday Planning
* **Focus:** Preventing annual debt traps caused by festive holiday cycles.
* **Topics:** Budgeting for Enkutatash (Ethiopian New Year), Meskel, Ganna (Christmas), Timket, Fasika (Easter), and Eid al-Fitr/Adha.

### Pillar D: Small Business Cashflow & Debt Isolation
* **Focus:** Helping kiosk and retail owners keep operational revenue separate from personal living expenses.
* **Topics:** Managing supplier payables, tracking customer micro-credit, and generating statements for bank loans.
